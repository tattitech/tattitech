🎬 Video Guide — Eh dekh ke samajh lo

1 Play Protect Off Karo

Pehla apne phone de Play Store vich jao, upar apni profile te click karo, Play Protect te jao, te Scan apps wala option Off kar do. (Eh zaroori aa, nahi ta app install nahi hogi).

2 App Install Karo

Hun apni Google Assistant APK download karo aur install kar lo. (Play Protect wapas On kar dena install hon de baad).

3 Restricted Settings Permission

Phone di Settings > Apps > Google Assistant vich jao. Ohde vich Restricted Settings da option hoga, ohnu Allow kar do. (Je na mile ta direct app kholo, oh apne aap permission mangega).

4 App Kholo Te Permissions Do

Hun app kholo (Google Assistant). Jdo oh SMS di permission mange, ta Allow kar do. (Notification te baaki permissions vi allow kar dena).

5 Naya Rule Banao (+ Icon)

App de vich niche + (plus) da icon hoga, ohde te click karo. Eh naya rule banao.

6 Name Te Number Likho

Ohde vich Name di jagah apna naam likho, te Number di jagah apna mobile number likho. (Jive: Maninder, 9876543210).

7 Add Te Click Karo

Bas! Hun niche ADD te click kar do. Tera kaam ho gaya. Hun jo vi SMS aayega, oh teri Telegram group vich aayega. ✅
